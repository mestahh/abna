# Product

## What it is
ABNA ("A Biztosítás Nem Alszik!" – insurance never sleeps) is the website of Szabó Balázs Márton,
a one-person insurance advisor in Tata, Hungary, who works with one international insurer.
Lines: home (lakás), compulsory motor + casco (KGFB), life and accident (élet és baleset).

## Audience
Households in Tata and the surrounding area (Komárom-Esztergom county), mostly arriving from
search or a personal recommendation, often on a phone. They are buying a person they can trust,
not a price-comparison engine.

## Job of the site
Make the advisor credible and reachable, then get a quote request or a phone call.
Primary actions: the three product quote forms (felmérők) and tap-to-call +36 30 360 3390.

## Brand commitments
- Name and slogan: ABNA, "A Biztosítás Nem Alszik!".
- Navy/blue identity carried over from the original site; Hungarian copy, formal "Ön" address.
- Real advisor photo and real local address as the main trust signals.
- No third-party trackers, fonts or scripts (GDPR; privacy notice says so).
